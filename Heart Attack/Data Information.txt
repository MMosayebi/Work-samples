CSV Notes:

* sex:
	- 0 : Female
	- 1 : Male

* cp: Chest pain type
	- 1 : Direct heart pain
	- 2 : Indirect pain that leads to feeling pain in the heart
	- 3 : Non-cardiac pain
	- 4 : without symptoms

* trtbps: Resting blood pressure

* chol: cholesterol

* fbs: High fasting blood sugar – diabetes
	- 0 : False
	- 1 : True

* rest_ecg: Resting ECG result
	- 0 : Normal
	- 1 : The ST segment of the ECG is abnormal
	- 2 : Probably or definitely the left ventricle is enlarged

* thalach: Maximum heart rate

* output: Heart attack chance
	- 0 : Low chance
	- 1 : High chance

* ca: Number of main vessels

* exang: Does the pain start with a specific activity?
	- 0 : False
	- 1 : True
